Effective Marketing

Turn real value into a reason to choose you.

Effective Marketing helps explain a supported capability to the right audience, reach that audience, and learn from the response. It connects positioning, proof, commercial copy, distribution, conversion, and sales without letting a persuasive story outrun the evidence.

01 / The range

What it covers

Positioning and proof
Segmentation, alternatives, categories, differentiation, message hierarchies, objections, and claim-to-proof checks.
Copy and conversion
Homepages, landing and pricing pages, comparison and alternatives pages, content refreshes, campaigns, sales and email copy, funnel diagnosis, and experiment decisions.
Content strategy
Audience questions, search intent, topic clusters, channel selection, production priorities, and content maintenance.
Launch and learning
Readiness conditions, sales enablement, growth options, rollout guardrails, win/loss reviews, and adoption analysis.
Service growth and sales
Service offers, bottleneck diagnosis, prospect research, outbound plans, qualification, consultation, objections, and useful follow-up.
Paid campaigns and CRM
Lead campaigns, retargeting, spend-to-sale measurement, pipeline definitions, saved views, and idempotent automations.
Social and professional presence
Platform-native content, LinkedIn posts and social selling, consultant profiles, evidence interviews, voice, and market adaptation.

02 / From request to result

How it works

  1. Establish what is true

    Identify the audience, the offer, available customer evidence, and the claims it can support.

  2. Make the market choice

    Choose the position, message, channel, or experiment and produce the material it needs.

  3. Define the feedback

    Name the signals that would change the decision and keep publication or spending authority explicit.

The result: A market-facing decision, message, or campaign grounded in what the product or service can prove.

03 / Ask in your own words

Try a real task.

Give the agent the relevant files, source material, and constraints. Start with an outcome like one of these:

  • We have three customer segments and no clear position. Give me two or three materially different options with the evidence and tradeoffs for each.

  • Signup drops 60% between the plan page and checkout. Diagnose it before proposing a test, and tell me if this is even an A/B test question.

  • We are a three-person IT service firm with enough inquiries but few sales. Find the bottleneck from inquiry to delivery and give me next week's plan.

04 / Choose the right discipline

When to use it

Use this skill for commercial communication and go-to-market decisions. Product research, pricing, and naming belong to Effective Product; editorial articles belong to Effective Writing; browser implementation belongs to Effective Web. The skill does not invent testimonials, results, or differentiation. Drafts do not authorize outreach, publishing, spending, or account changes.

For the complete workflow and routing rules, read the agent instructions on GitHub ↗.

05 / Add this discipline

Install Effective Marketing

With Node.js installed, run this command for an agent that supports Agent Skills:

npx skills add sebastian-software/effective-agent --skill effective-marketing

Then describe your task in the agent. This skill works on its own; add another when the work crosses disciplines.
