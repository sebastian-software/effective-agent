The range

What it covers

The current route is Business Portrait Retouch. Further image workflows can be added as separate routes.

Clothing
Smooth distracting sleeve, shoulder, and chest folds while keeping fabric, garment outlines, and necessary pose folds.
Faces and glasses
Reduce distracting glare, balance facial shadows, and soften isolated blemishes while keeping real expressions and skin texture.
Series finishing
Apply a coherent approved color treatment with natural skin, gentle contrast, and quieter competing background highlights.
Comparison and review
Show before/after views, inspect identity and scene details, and report actual output dimensions.

From request to result

How it works

  1. Inspect and establish the look

    Use supplied originals and an existing brief. Show representative proposals when requested, then carry an approved look through the authorized series.

  2. Declare reference roles

    Separate the edit target from a color-only or identity-only reference so unrelated people and objects do not enter the scene.

  3. Compare and deliver

    Review against each original and across the series. Save separate outputs, record prompts, and report the actual resolution.

The result: A welcoming professional portrait that keeps the character of the original shoot.

Ask in your own words

Try a real task.

  • Show before/after proposals for my business shoot. Smooth the shirt folds and reduce glasses glare while keeping natural faces.

  • Apply this approved warm look to the remaining portraits. Keep the poses and smiles, but soften tired-looking facial shadows.

  • Refine this approved edit using the original only for identity and anatomy. Keep its existing color treatment.

Choose the right skill

When to use it

Use the Business Portrait Retouch route for Imagegen editing of existing business portraits and photo-shoot series. The current route does not cover new scenes or body reshaping. Commercial copy and CDN administration belong outside this skill. Output dimensions depend on the available editing tool; private photographs are not included in the package.

Install Effective Image

With Node.js installed, run this command for an agent that supports Agent Skills:

npx skills add sebastian-software/skills.sebastian-software.com --skill effective-image

Then describe your task in the agent. This skill works on its own; add another when the work crosses disciplines.
