01 / The range

What it covers

Three routes, Photo Editing, Business Portrait Retouch, and Photo Generation, share one realism review.

Clothing
Smooth distracting sleeve, shoulder, and chest folds while keeping fabric, garment outlines, and necessary pose folds.
Faces and glasses
Reduce distracting glare, balance facial shadows, remove blemishes, and give a subtly fresher, fitter look while keeping real expressions, identity, and skin texture.
Series finishing
Apply a coherent approved color treatment with natural skin, gentle contrast, and quieter competing background highlights.
Edits and composites
Clean up photos, change backgrounds, and insert real products with short single-change edits that match light and perspective.
New photoreal scenes
Direct new scenes and brand visuals with a fixed series brief, concrete photographic language, and deliberate realism cues.
Realism review
Catch plastic skin, sterile light, perfect symmetry, and weightless objects; inspect identity and details against the source and report actual output dimensions.

02 / From request to result

How it works

  1. Inspect and establish the look

    Use supplied originals and an existing brief. Show representative proposals when requested, then carry an approved look through the authorized series.

  2. Start from the original

    Every version starts from the untouched original; an approved edit serves only as a color reference, so unrelated people and objects do not enter the scene.

  3. Compare and deliver

    Review against each original and across the series. Save separate outputs, record prompts, and report the actual resolution.

The result: A welcoming professional portrait that keeps the character of the original shoot.

03 / Ask in your own words

Try a real task.

  • Show before/after proposals for my business shoot. Smooth the shirt folds and reduce glasses glare while keeping natural faces.

  • Apply this approved warm look to the remaining portraits. Keep the poses and smiles, but soften tired-looking facial shadows.

  • Redo this portrait from the original so it looks fresher. Keep the quiet sideways gaze and the approved colors.

  • Create a warm travertine bathroom scene for our perfume bottle that looks like an actual photograph, not an AI render.

04 / Choose the right skill

When to use it

Use Photo Editing for changes to existing photos, Business Portrait Retouch for business portraits and photo-shoot series, and Photo Generation for new photoreal scenes and brand visuals. Illustration, stylized art, and visible body reshaping are not covered. Commercial copy and CDN administration belong outside this skill. Output dimensions depend on the available editing tool; private photographs are not included in the package.
