01 / The range

What it covers

Five routes carry a film from brief to published player: Film Plan, Remotion Picture, Voiceover, Score and Mix, and Delivery.

Film plan
Turn the brief into a beat sheet on a musical bar grid, with checkpoints before each expensive render.
Remotion picture
Build scenes and camera moves in code, choose between your own components and copied snapcn components, apply the brand theme, and check frames.
Voiceover
Use license-checked open text-to-speech such as Chatterbox or Kokoro, never clone a real person’s voice, keep takes seeded, and check pronunciation.
Score and mix
Write an original MIDI score rendered with licensed soundfonts, duck it under the voice, and master the mix to −16 LUFS.
Delivery
Keep video files out of Git, publish a click-to-play Bunny Stream embed, use YouTube for reach, and archive the originals.
Captions and poster
Ship captions and a poster frame with every published film so it reads without sound and before playback.

02 / From request to result

How it works

  1. Plan on the bar grid

    Agree on the brief and length, then lay out beats on a musical bar grid so picture, voice, and score share one timeline.

  2. Build picture and sound

    Render Remotion scenes, generate seeded voiceover takes, and compose an original score; check frames and pronunciation at each checkpoint.

  3. Mix, render, and deliver

    Duck and master the mix, render the film, add captions and a poster, publish the streaming embed, and archive the originals outside Git.

The result: A finished product film you can reproduce from its source and replace one take at a time.

03 / Ask in your own words

Try a real task.

  • Make a 60-second product film for our homepage with an English voiceover and calm piano music.

  • Rebuild the promo with snapcn components where they fit, but keep our product scenes.

  • The voice says ‘Dahlo’ at 0:15 — fix that take without touching the rest.

  • Publish the film on Bunny Stream with a click-to-play player and archive the originals.

04 / Choose the right skill

When to use it

Use Effective Video for promo, launch, and demo films rendered from code. Live-action shooting, editing camera footage, and ad buying are not covered. Messaging, copy, and positioning belong to Effective Marketing; the code that embeds the film in a site belongs to Effective Web. Voice and music tools must have licenses that allow your use; the package ships no rendered films.
