# Photo Generation

Use for new photoreal images: scenes, brand and lifestyle visuals, product
settings, and series that must look like one shoot. Changing an existing
photograph, including placing a real product into a finished image, follows
[Photo Editing](route-photo-editing.md).

## Direct Before Prompting

Settle the image's intent before writing a prompt: the mood it should carry,
the light, the setting and styling, and the camera angle and focus. A series
needs one fixed brief: the same camera and lens, lighting approach, palette,
and recurring materials. Reuse that vocabulary for every image instead of
rewriting prompts from scratch, or the series drifts.

## Generation Prompts

A generation prompt is an art director's brief:

- Put the subject first; models weight early words more heavily.
- Continue with key details, setting and materials, mood, light, composition,
  and lens, one idea per short phrase.
- Be concrete: light type and direction ("soft window light from the left,
  gentle shadow"), real materials, a named composition, a focal length.
- Describe the desired state instead of negations; "white seamless background
  with negative space" works better than "no clutter".
- Keep style keywords few and coherent, roughly five to seven; contradictory
  modifiers average into a generic look.
- Start with subject and light, inspect, then layer detail in variations so
  each change stays attributable.

## Add Realism Deliberately

Generated images default to plastic skin, sterile light, and perfect symmetry.
After the base prompt works, add a second layer with the few imperfections
this specific scene would really carry, chosen from the
[Realism Review](realism-review.md): material history, optical behavior, and
organic irregularity. A fixed list of film effects on every image is its own
kind of artificiality.

## Real Products and People

A real product, logo, or person enters a generated scene as a source image,
never as a description. Generate the scene first, then insert the real item
with an edit pass from [Photo Editing](route-photo-editing.md) so its shape,
label, and identity stay exact. Do not generate likenesses of real people from
text alone.
