# Competitive Profiles

Build comparable, dated evidence on alternatives for a specific decision.
Profiles support product, positioning, sales, or commercial-page work; they do
not decide the position or turn unverified claims into publishable facts.

## Bound the investigation

Name the decision, audience and use case, market boundary, alternatives, and
evidence cutoff. Include in-house work, manual processes, or doing nothing when
they are genuine alternatives. Choose depth by decision risk, uncertainty,
deadline, and access rather than the number of competitors.

A quick profile can establish relevant positioning, documented capabilities,
public plan conditions, and open questions. Deeper work may add hands-on tests,
buyer evidence, switching constraints, or licensed demand data when these could
change the decision. Explain which dimensions were investigated and which
remain unknown; a filled template does not imply exhaustive research.

## Collect comparable evidence

Use a shared set of decision-relevant dimensions across the alternatives:

- audience, use case, category, and the vendor's stated promise;
- capabilities, dependencies, limits, and the plan or version they apply to;
- price, billing period, currency, region, seat or usage basis, minimums,
  add-ons, and material contract conditions;
- onboarding, interoperability, switching requirements, support, and access
  constraints when relevant;
- available proof, buyer-reported experiences, and unresolved contradictions;
- distribution or demand signals only when the decision needs them.

Separate official claims and documented terms from observed behavior, customer
reports, and analyst interpretation. Record source, observation date, applicable
plan or version, and evidence limits for consequential facts. Preserve a source
publication date where known; observation today does not make an old report
current. Verify volatile facts at use time.

Make comparisons on equivalent terms. Do not rank a monthly per-user plan
against an annual flat price without stating the different conditions. Mark
unavailable information as unknown rather than zero, absent, or weak. Check
apparently conflicting claims against plan, region, version, and observation
date before deciding they truly conflict.

For primary-source selection, public reviews, community evidence, access,
freshness, and bias, use
[Market and source research](market-and-source-research.md). Do not manufacture
customer numbers, revenue, roadmap commitments, or satisfaction from website
copy, a few reviews, or third-party traffic estimates.

## Use optional demand data carefully

Search rankings, keyword estimates, backlinks, paid placements, and traffic
estimates can suggest distribution patterns or research questions. Use available
authorized tools and data; no provider is mandatory. Preserve provider, period,
market, and metric definitions, and label estimates.

These measures are not interchangeable with usage, customer count, revenue,
market share, product quality, or causal acquisition performance. If access is
missing, deliver the supported profile and name the gap. Do not acquire a new
subscription or scrape restricted sources merely to complete a template.

## Synthesize and hand off

Return comparable profiles or a decision matrix, source references and dates,
material unknowns, and a synthesis that separates evidence from interpretation.
Explain supported strengths, constraints, and fit by audience. Do not declare a
universal winner or manufacture our differentiation.

For repeated work, use an existing research record or one shared fact basis so
sales and commercial pages do not drift. Preserve prior observations and a
concise change note when history matters; do not overwrite an old price as if
it had always been current. Revalidate on changes to plans, product behavior,
contracts, or a consequential downstream claim, with frequency proportionate
to volatility and risk.

Store raw material only when requested or required by the repository's research
practice and permitted by access, consent, licensing, and retention rules. A
small request may need only a sourced response; no root-level folder, paid tool,
or mandatory artifact set is required.

Hand positioning, message, comparison-page, and sales implications to
`effective-marketing`. Keep product scope and pricing decisions on their own
routes. Use [Synthesis and handoffs](synthesis-and-handoffs.md) when the work
needs a broader evidence register or owner-specific research handoff.
