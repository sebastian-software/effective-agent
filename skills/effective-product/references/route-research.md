# Route: Customer and Market Research

Turn an important uncertainty into a proportionate research program, traceable
evidence, and a decision-ready handoff. Keep the research function independent
enough to contradict the preferred product, design, or market story.

## Workflow

1. Name the decision contract:
   - decision, decision owner, deadline, and consequence of being wrong
   - target people, situation, geography, market boundary, and exclusions
   - current beliefs, supporting evidence, contradictions, and unknowns
   - what finding could change, stop, narrow, or accelerate the decision
2. Inventory available evidence before collecting more. Separate direct
   observation, participant accounts, measured behavior, commercial commitment,
   internal records, public or licensed sources, team interpretation, and
   assumptions. Record source, date, context, selection mechanism, and access
   limits.
3. Load only the matching reference:
   - [Program design and fieldwork](program-design-and-fieldwork.md) for method
     selection, sampling, recruitment, screeners, interviews, observation,
     surveys, participant operations, consent, and data handling.
   - [Market and source research](market-and-source-research.md) for category
     and alternative mapping, competitor-customer evidence, public and licensed
     sources, reviews, communities, demand signals, and market sizing.
   - [Competitive profiles](competitive-profiles.md) for comparable, dated
     dossiers on alternatives, their plans, constraints, and changes over time.
   - [Synthesis and handoffs](synthesis-and-handoffs.md) for evidence registers,
     coding, themes, contradictions, Voice of Customer, Jobs to Be Done,
     personas, confidence, research repositories, and owner-specific handoffs.
4. Write a research brief before fieldwork or broad searching. Match each
   question to a method, source, participant or unit, analysis approach,
   evidence limit, and affected decision. Remove questions whose answers would
   not change anything.
5. Execute with traceability. Preserve raw material where access, consent, and
   retention rules permit; keep notes and excerpts linked to their origin; log
   exclusions, failed recruitment, missing perspectives, and method changes.
6. Analyze observations before implications. Test themes against negative
   cases, segment differences, source bias, alternative explanations, and any
   measured behavior available. Report exact denominators instead of implying
   prevalence from a convenient sample.
7. Produce the smallest useful research artifact and hand it to the decision
   owner. Separate findings, interpretation, implications, recommendations for
   further research, and decisions that remain outside research.
8. Close the loop with unresolved uncertainty, evidence freshness, repository
   location, access and deletion obligations, next research trigger, and the
   condition that would reopen the finding.

## Operating Rules

- Research a decision, not a topic in the abstract. Broad discovery is
  legitimate only when it ends in bounded questions and named downstream
  choices.
- Never invent participants, quotes, observations, demand, prevalence,
  competitor behavior, market size, willingness to pay, or confidence.
- Prefer specific past behavior, observed context, actual alternatives,
  switching, payment, adoption, and retention over hypothetical intent.
- Keep buyer, user, administrator, approver, blocker, and payer perspectives
  distinct when their incentives or experience differ.
- Treat interviews, observation, surveys, behavioral data, reviews, support
  records, sales notes, communities, analyst reports, and search data as
  different evidence channels with different selection effects.
- Use no universal minimum sample, confidence label, recency window, or
  saturation threshold. Scale rigor to method, variation, risk, reversibility,
  access, and the cost of a wrong decision.
- Quote only material that is supplied or lawfully accessible, preserve enough
  context to avoid changing its meaning, and do not turn public availability
  into permission for unsafe personal-data reuse.
- Verify volatile market, competitor, platform, price, and regulatory facts at
  the time of use. Preserve source and observation dates.
- Use AI to organize supplied material, expose contradictory interpretations,
  draft provisional instruments, and accelerate source review. Do not let it
  simulate respondents or fill evidence gaps.
- Make participation voluntary and accessible. Preserve consent, withdrawal,
  recording, confidentiality, incentive, access, retention, and deletion
  decisions.

## Default Deliverable

Return the smallest decision-ready package containing:

1. decision contract, scope, owner, evidence cutoff, and learning goals;
2. existing-evidence inventory with important contradictions and gaps;
3. method and source plan with selection logic and known biases;
4. recruitment, fieldwork, consent, and data-handling plan when applicable;
5. evidence register linked to raw material where permitted;
6. findings, negative cases, segment differences, and confidence limits;
7. implications and handoffs separated by decision owner;
8. unresolved questions, next research trigger, and repository obligations.

## Cross-links

- The target market, product thesis, outcomes, viability, scope, priorities,
  roadmap, release, and product experiment are decided on the Discovery,
  Strategy, and Scope routes. Receive their decision and uncertainty; return
  market, customer, commitment, and alternative evidence without making the
  product decision.
- Experience questions, behavioral patterns, mental models, system and
  interaction models, prototypes, and usability findings belong to the Design
  Research and Design Modeling routes. Run the broader recruitment, fieldwork,
  participant operations, and repository work here.
- Willingness-to-pay research design and the pricing decision itself are the
  Pricing route. This route may execute a supplied research plan but does not
  infer a price from generic sentiment or competitor lists.
- Segment, category, positioning, messaging, claim, proof, launch, and
  market-learning decisions, and funnel diagnosis or conversion experiments,
  belong to `effective-marketing`. Return buyer language, decision criteria,
  alternatives, win/loss evidence, and proof gaps without manufacturing
  differentiation.
- Web-facing requirements for cookies, tracking, testimonials, direct-marketing
  capture, and online research disclosures belong to `effective-web`'s
  compliance route; jurisdiction-specific rules for recruitment, recording,
  participant personal data, and incentives need the organization's privacy or
  legal owner.
- This route does not replace formal statistical, legal, regulated
  human-subjects, scientific, or specialist industry review when the decision
  exceeds the available competence and evidence.
