# Platform Adaptation

Keep the idea and evidence stable while adapting the reading context,
conversation model, media role, and expected interaction.

## Start with Current Reality

Platform limits, post types, ranking systems, link treatment, editing,
accessibility controls, API access, and moderation rules change. Verify current
official guidance when an exact capability or limit determines the deliverable.
Do not turn remembered character counts, timing advice, hashtag counts, or
algorithm folklore into requirements.

When exact limits do not matter, optimize for a complete thought, readable
structure, and the user's sustainable publishing workflow.

## Adapt by Channel

### X or Twitter

Favor one sharp, self-contained thought, a proportionate reply, a useful quote
post, or a thread whose sequence is genuinely necessary. Let concrete nouns,
results, questions, and mechanisms carry the post. Do not force every idea into
compressed hot-take language.

For threads, make the opening establish the governing idea and let later parts
advance it. For replies, respond to the conversation rather than rewriting the
speaker's standalone-post voice at smaller scale.

For an X content program, cadence choice, algorithm-claim review, or strategy
update, use the dated [X strategy playbook](x-strategy-playbook.md). Keep its
durable baseline separate from the volatile implementation snapshot.

### Threads

Use conversational context and natural continuity when they help. A post can
feel spoken without becoming vague or rambling. Preserve a clear point and
avoid assuming that informality excuses unsupported claims.

When adapting from X, reconsider whether the idea benefits from a little more
context or a more conversational ending instead of merely copying the text.

### Bluesky

Keep the post understandable within the immediate conversation and community
context. Favor clear attribution, specific observations, and useful replies.
Do not invent a universal "Bluesky voice" or assume that one community's norms
apply to every audience.

### Mastodon

Account for the norms and moderation context of the relevant instance when
known. Use content warnings, alt text, and visibility choices when the actual
subject or community calls for them. Do not assume one instance represents the
whole network.

### Instagram

Treat the visual, carousel, or video as the primary content surface when the
idea is visual. Let the caption add context, interpretation, attribution, or a
next step rather than duplicating every frame.

For carousels:

1. establish the promise or question
2. sequence one idea per frame where practical
3. show examples, contrast, or evidence
4. close with the implication or useful next step

For video, provide the hook, scene or demonstration sequence, spoken or
on-screen wording, caption role, subtitles, and relevant accessibility notes.
Do not prescribe a universal duration without current evidence and a reason.

### LinkedIn

Route a LinkedIn-only request to the LinkedIn posts route, which owns its ideas,
professional context, document formats, and post-writing guidance.

For a multi-platform assignment:

1. establish the shared idea, audience, evidence, voice, and claim boundary
   with the social content route;
2. let the LinkedIn posts route compose the LinkedIn version;
3. keep other platform versions here;
4. compare the set for factual consistency without forcing identical openings,
   pacing, or endings.

## Adapt One Idea Across Platforms

Use a shared brief:

- governing idea
- intended audience and desired change
- evidence and approved proof
- speaker and voice boundaries
- commercial connection or explicit lack of one
- terminology, confidentiality, and claim constraints
- source link or media assets

Then assign each version a channel job. For example:

| Platform context | Possible job |
| --- | --- |
| Fast conversational feed | Land one sharp observation or question |
| Reply context | Add one reason, example, constraint, or useful question |
| Thread or series | Teach a sequence or unfold a supported argument |
| Visual-first feed | Demonstrate, compare, or sequence visually |
| Professional network | Connect the lesson to credible work and useful discussion |

These are selection cues, not platform laws. Choose the job from the actual
audience, source material, and goal.

Recompose:

- opening and amount of setup
- paragraph or sequence shape
- terminology explanation
- link and attribution placement
- media and caption relationship
- call to action or conversational ending

Keep qualifications intact when shortening, and never present a format,
cadence, or posting time as a reach guarantee.

## Preserve Accessibility and Integrity

- Provide meaningful alt text or an alt-text brief for informative images.
- Caption spoken video and identify important non-speech information.
- Keep on-image text readable at mobile sizes and repeat essential meaning in
  accessible text when needed.
- Preserve source attribution and sponsored, affiliate, endorsement, or
  promotion disclosures required by the actual relationship and jurisdiction.
- Do not obscure material conditions in an image, final thread reply, collapsed
  caption, or inaccessible media layer.
