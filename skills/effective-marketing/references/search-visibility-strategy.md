# Search Visibility Strategy

Treat SEO and GEO as overlapping ways to help the intended audience find,
understand, and choose a supported offer. A citation is an observation about one
answer, not proof of demand, recommendation, traffic, or revenue.

## Find the opportunity before choosing the tactic

Use actual customer questions, support records, sales objections, search-query
exports, cited pages, and current search results. Record source, date, locale,
and sample limits. Label suggested queries as hypotheses when customer evidence
is missing. Never fabricate search volumes, rankings, citations, or conversions.

Group questions by audience task and intent, then map them to existing assets.
Inspect current results when page format or intent affects the choice. Result
overlap can inform consolidation, but a fixed top-ten overlap threshold is not
a universal rule. Do not make one page per keyword or every query an assistant
generates; retrieval queries are clues to investigate, not automatic demand.

Prioritize relevance to the offer, useful evidence available, realistic
distribution access, technical dependencies, and maintenance cost. Compare
refreshing an existing page with creating a new one. Route crawl or indexing
failures to `effective-web` before interpreting weak traffic as a content gap.

For a new domain, establish the technical baseline and a small set of useful
pages. Do not prescribe a universal weekly publishing quota or wait a fixed
number of months to correct an observable defect. For an established site, use
actual query and conversion evidence to choose improvements; distinguish a
dated demand shift from an implementation regression or seasonal change.

## Commission pages that help a decision

Useful candidates include comparisons, alternatives, pricing explanations,
implementation guidance, and pages for a specific audience situation. Choose
them because the task and evidence warrant them, not because a format is
supposed to be favored by every answer engine.

For each proposed page, specify the audience question, current result pattern,
existing asset to improve or replace, distinctive evidence, and next useful
action. Answer the question directly with enough context to stand alone. Do
not impose a fixed word count, number of options, keyword density, or score.

For comparisons:

- Disclose the publisher's commercial relationship and selection criteria.
- Compare options for the same use case on dated, comparable terms. Verify
  prices, limitations, and capabilities against primary sources; distinguish
  vendor claims from firsthand tests and unknowns.
- Explain material tradeoffs, including limitations of the publisher's offer.
  Do not invent drawbacks or force a ranking to look neutral.
- Add useful original evidence where available: a reproducible test, annotated
  screenshot, implementation example, or measured result with its method.

Use **Marketing Copywriting** for the resulting commercial page. Technical
publication and verification go to `effective-web`; informative articles go
to `effective-writing`.

## Distribute facts and correct inaccurate answers

Maintain a dated fact record for consequential claims such as product name,
plans, prices, supported platforms, eligibility, and availability. Give each
fact a source, scope, owner, and review trigger. Reuse an existing product or
business source of truth rather than creating a competing one.

Compare sampled AI answers against that record. Capture engine, date, query,
incorrect claim, cited URL, and supporting source. Trace the error to the
actual source when possible; an answer's citation does not prove that source
contains the wrong statement. Correct owned content and structured data through
`effective-web`; request third-party corrections when authorized. For uncited
or untraceable errors, record that limit rather than inventing a source.

Retest the same question and relevant variants across a proportionate period.
Record observed correction separately from confirmed source updates. A clean
answer on one run does not establish that an error has disappeared everywhere;
choose the review interval for the impact and recurrence, not a universal
two-month threshold.

Select third-party surfaces the audience actually uses: relevant directories,
partner documentation, reviews, communities, or video. Keep identity and facts
consistent. Distinguish a proposed placement from a secured mention. Avoid
paid link schemes, fabricated reviews, undisclosed promotion, doorway pages,
and prompt injection aimed at crawlers. Do not treat directory count as a KPI.

## Measure enough to change a decision

Define the desired outcome and baseline before choosing a tracking product.
Separate entity mentions, linked citations, factual accuracy, referral visits,
and qualified outcomes. Preserve denominators and report counts for sparse
samples; avoid presenting noisy percentages as a trend.

Use a repeatable panel of real audience questions and retain answer snapshots.
Have `effective-web` define provider-specific sampling and instrumentation via
its AI/GEO guidance. Keep engine, locale, query, personalization state where
controllable, and date comparable. Repeated samples reduce dependence on one
answer; they do not establish market-wide visibility or causality.

Use available first-party search reports alongside analytics and logs. Bing's
AI Performance report covers supported Microsoft and partner surfaces, not
every assistant; its sampled grounding queries are not keyword volumes.
Verify current coverage in [Bing's documentation](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/).
Provider reporting availability is time-sensitive; this scope was checked on
November 11, 2026.

Pair citation trends with acquisition and outcome evidence. Optional
self-reported acquisition can complement referrers but introduces recall and
collection bias. Do not add the two as independent conversions or attribute
all branded search to GEO. Choose a review point based on traffic, sales cycle,
and impact; keep, revise, or stop work based on the stated goal and evidence.
