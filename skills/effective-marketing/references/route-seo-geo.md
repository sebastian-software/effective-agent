# Route: SEO and AI Search Visibility

Use this route to decide which search opportunities to pursue, which pages or
reputation surfaces to improve, and whether classic search or generative engine
optimization (GEO) work is producing useful results.

## Workflow

1. Establish the audience, offer, target markets, available evidence, and desired
   outcome. Distinguish a new domain without history from a site with usable
   query, citation, and conversion data.
2. Read [Search visibility strategy](search-visibility-strategy.md) to prioritize
   audience questions, decision pages, distribution, factual corrections, and
   measurement. Use [Content Strategy](route-content-strategy.md) when the work
   needs a broader portfolio or channel decision.
3. Hand technical symptoms or implementation to `effective-web` through its
   **Frontend SEO and AI Search** route. Supply the target URLs, intended facts,
   audience tasks, and observed failures; request live evidence where access is
   available. Do not diagnose indexability from a citation score alone.
4. Return a prioritized plan with the question or failure each item addresses,
   supporting evidence or assumption, intended page or surface, dependency, and
   observation that would change the decision. Produce requested drafts or
   carry an authorized combined plan-and-fix request through the handoff.

## Boundaries and handoffs

- Search opportunity selection, commercial content priorities, off-site
  distribution, answer-accuracy review, and interpretation of outcomes stay here.
- `effective-web` owns metadata, redirects, canonicals, structured data,
  internal-link implementation, crawler/CDN diagnosis, release checks, and
  measurement instrumentation. Its AI/GEO reference owns provider behavior
  and the repeatable query-panel specification; use it when available rather
  than inventing universal crawler rules or a visibility score.
- Draft comparison or alternatives copy through [Marketing Copywriting](route-copywriting.md).
  Editorial explainers go to `effective-writing`; new customer or competitor
  fieldwork goes to `effective-product`.
- A strategy request alone does not authorize outreach or publication. Preserve
  authority already granted for implementation; do not add approval stops before
  every routine edit in an authorized workflow.
