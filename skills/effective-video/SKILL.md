# Effective Video

A film is one artifact on one timeline. Picture cuts, voice lines, and music
bars share a single grid, so settle the plan before rendering anything, and
change timing in one place rather than nudging each layer separately.

Work in checkpoints the requester can judge by eye and ear: voice samples,
then an animatic with the real timing, then the final render. Report what was
only measured, not heard or watched, as such.

## Route by Intent

| User intent | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Turn a product and a message into a brief, beat sheet, length, format, and checkpoints | [Film Plan](references/route-film-plan.md) |
| Build or fix scenes, camera moves, transitions, or brand look in Remotion, including snapcn components | [Remotion Picture](references/route-remotion-picture.md) |
| Create, replace, or fix a synthetic voiceover, including the pronunciation of a name | [Voiceover](references/route-voiceover.md) |
| Write an original score, place it under the voice, or fix loudness and balance | [Score and Mix](references/route-score-mix.md) |
| Render masters, captions, and a poster; publish to streaming or video platforms; archive originals | [Delivery](references/route-delivery.md) |

A request for a whole film starts with the Film Plan and moves through the
other routes in order. A fix to one finished film goes straight to its route.

## Routing Boundaries

The message, positioning, script claims, and channel strategy belong to
`effective-marketing`; this skill owns their timing, beat structure, and
wording for synthesis. Embed code and player UI on a website belong to
`effective-web`. Still photographs belong to `effective-image`.

Live-action shooting, editing camera footage, and paid media are not covered.
Do not clone a real person's voice, and do not reproduce copyrighted music
"in the style of" by copying its melodies. Uploading to accounts, storage, or
channels requires its own destination and authorization; this skill alone
does not grant either.
